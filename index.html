Forge

Design fleets, make them fight, see what wins. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

⚙ Fleet GeneratorPrice, ships, a coat of plating and colours, grand tactics — a fleet as one code you can copy. ⚔ Battle LabPaste two fleet codes and watch them fight; or random ships at one price, who wins, the challenger, generations. ✎ PainterDraw silhouettes and dress them in plating styles; your shapes join the lab's random ships. ◈ Eon prototypeThe 4X loop: focus, research, places, designs, a space dock — the game this is all for.
How to share a fleet: open the Fleet Generator, compose, press ⧉ Copy the code, send the text to a friend. They paste it into the Battle Lab as Fleet 1 or Fleet 2 and press ⚔ Fight the codes. A code carries the fleet's coat and any silhouettes you drew, so it works on any browser.
