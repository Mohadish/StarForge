Greeble Lab the styles' parts as a stock of greebles — and the technologies as pieces of hardware

Every style's sheet, cut: the whole pieces trimmed to their ink, panels and bands off the plating strip, and tiles of whole fittings layered. Devices are solid blocks in the strip's texture and CARDS — the whole greebles as flat pieces in space, their outline overhanging: flanges, fins, plates, pillars, discs. Devices: a technology as hardware, its level a colour (red → orange → yellow → green → blue → purple → violet → black with white outlines). Hover a device to turn it.
